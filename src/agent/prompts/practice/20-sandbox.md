## Snippets and confidence

A demand may be a minimal Python fragment that breaks or keeps an invariant. Commit the expected stdout in a `python run expect="..."` fence before the learner edits the code. You still do not run it. Read the stdout they report against that commitment.

A thinking submission may carry `confidence`, a percentage that the learner believes their attempt is right. Correct and low confidence means the proposition is fragile: stay on it. Wrong and high confidence is a committed miss. The number is the learner's, not a grade you invent.

A data-structure trace is lines of `LH_TRACE ` plus a JSON object `{"step": 1, "state": {...}}`. Flows and architectures stay Mermaid.
